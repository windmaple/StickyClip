click a note to copy it — or press

Your clipboard history,
hung out on a line.

StickyClip is a tiny menu-bar clipboard manager for macOS. Press ⇧⌘V and everything you've copied drops down as colourful sticky notes. Pick one and it's pasted right where you were working.

Download for macOS View source on GitHub

Free & open source · macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple silicon & Intel

Hang them however you like

Pick the board that suits your desk.

The clothesline strings a rope between the two ends of your menu bar, and windows in the way slide down to make room. Rather have something more grounded? Switch styles from the menu bar at any time.

Small app, sensible habits

Everything you copy, one keystroke away.

  • Text, links, images & files

    Real thumbnails for images, PDFs and videos you copy in Finder — not just a filename.

  • Never steals focus

    ⇧⌘V opens the picker on top of what you're doing. Pick with ↩ or ⌘1–⌘9, or just start typing to search.

  • Drag onto any window

    Drop a note where you want it. Apps that don't accept drops get brought forward and pasted into instead.

  • Pin the keepers

    Pinned notes are never pushed out of history. Copy the same thing twice and it simply moves to the top.

  • Skips your passwords

    Respects the concealed and transient markers that password managers set, so secrets never land on a note.

  • Lives in the menu bar

    No Dock icon. History size, auto-paste, pause recording and launch at login are all a click away.

Hands stay on the keyboard

Built for muscle memory.

Click a note to paste it, right-click for more, or click anywhere outside to put the board away. Every action also has a key.

Show or hide the board
←→Tab

↩

⇧↩

⌘1–9

⌘⌫

esc
Clear the search, or close

Your clipboard never leaves your Mac.

No network access

History is stored on disk in ~/Library/Application Support/StickyClip/ and nowhere else.

No screen recording

Window shifting reads window bounds only. Screen pixels are never captured.

Accessibility is optional

It enables auto-paste and window shifting. Without it, picking a note copies it so you can press ⌘V yourself.

Up and running in a minute

Install StickyClip.

Download the latest release, unzip it, and drag StickyClip.app into Applications. Then turn on Launch at Login from the menu bar icon.

Download StickyClip.app.zip

  1. First launch blocked?

    Release builds aren't notarized yet. Control-click the app and choose Open, or clear the quarantine flag:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/StickyClip.app

  2. Or build it yourself

    Needs macOS 14+ and the Xcode command-line tools.

    git clone https://github.com/windmaple/StickyClip && cd StickyClip && ./build_app.sh && open StickyClip.app
